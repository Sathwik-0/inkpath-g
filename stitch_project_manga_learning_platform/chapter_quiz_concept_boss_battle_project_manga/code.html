Concept Analysis: Copenhagen Reduction vs Unitary Time Evolution

Standard quantum mechanics dictates that while an unobserved quantum system evolves unitarily according to the Schrödinger equation, any intervention by a classical or macroscopic measurement instrument breaks unitarity. The superposition collapses probabilistically to an observable eigenvalue according to the Born Rule ($P(i) = |\langle \psi_i | \Psi \rangle|^2$). In Chapter 3, Jin Woo-Hyun leverages this sudden wavefunction collapse to destabilize the dimensional barrier, physically enforcing a single deterministic reality.
